How I use AI

Yes, I use AI tools to help build the things on this site. Here's why, and what that does and doesn't mean.

On this pageWhy I use itWhat AI does hereWhat it doesn't meanWhat it means for youIf this bothers you

The short version

I design, test and use everything I release. AI helps me through the parts I'd never otherwise have time for, so projects actually get finished, and bugs and suggestions actually get answered.

Why I use it

I love starting projects and digging into the interesting parts. Finishing them is another story: my brain just doesn't reward the last stretch the way it rewards the first. Add a full-time job and a family, and there's never been time to push through the tedious parts, or to learn every skill it takes to finish and publish something.

Some of these projects have been rattling around in my head, or sitting half-done in a projects folder, for nearly ten years. AI is what's finally getting them finished and out into the world for people to use and enjoy.

What AI does here

It varies a lot from project to project.

  • Time Calc is at one end. I designed it, made its artwork, and wrote and tested nearly all of its code. AI helped with the newer pieces: the ads, in-app purchases, getting it ready for the app stores, and this website.
  • My Minecraft mods are at the other end. I came up with what each one should do and how it should look. AI wrote the code and made the art, one step at a time, with my feedback after every step. I tested everything in the game.
  • Everything else falls somewhere in between. Typical jobs: writing and reviewing code, tracking down bugs, build and release chores, and drafting things like the privacy policy.

What doesn't change is that the ideas, the design decisions and the final say are always mine. I test everything and use it myself, and nothing ships until I'm happy with it. I may not have placed every pixel by hand, but every one of them is there because I wanted it there.

What it doesn't mean

This isn't a slop factory. Nothing here was generated and shipped. Everything I release has been through many hours of my own design, testing, rework and everyday use.

No one lost a paycheck. I don't have the money to hire designers, artists or other developers, and never have. Without AI, these projects would never have been finished, or would have been finished with a lot less polish.

What it means for you

No one person catches everything, even with help, so when you report a bug or suggest an idea, I actually have the time to do something about it.

If this bothers you

I know AI is a sore subject for a lot of people, and you're free to pass on anything here. But if something caught your interest enough to bring you to this site, then I hope my use of AI won't keep you from enjoying the work I put into it.
