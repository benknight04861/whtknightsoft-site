The short version

  • I don't collect your name, email or anything you type into my apps.
  • Some apps send anonymous usage stats and crash reports so I can see what's used and fix bugs.
  • Free versions show ads, and the ad networks collect device information to show them.
  • Payments go through Google Play or the App Store; I never see them.

What this covers

This policy covers every app, game and mod published by Wht Knight Software, and this website. Where something applies to only one app, it says so.

What my apps keep

Nothing you type or calculate ever leaves your device unless you copy or share it yourself. Your settings are saved only on the device, and they're deleted when you uninstall the app.

I don't ask for your name, email or any account, and I don't sell or share anything about you.

Usage stats and crash reports

Some apps (Time Calc included) send basic usage numbers and crash reports to Unity, the company whose tools the apps are built with. This tells me how many people use an app and helps me fix crashes.

It includes things like when the app was opened and for how long, your device model and operating system version, and what went wrong if the app crashed. It's tied to a random ID created when the app is installed, not to you. It never includes your name, email, contacts, or anything you type into the app.

Ads in free versions

Free versions show ads through Unity LevelPlay, which picks ads from Unity Ads and other ad networks. To show, count and pay for those ads, and to stop the same ad appearing too often, these companies may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address, and a rough location worked out from it (country or city, not your exact position)
  • device details such as model, operating system and language
  • which ads you saw and tapped

On iPhone and iPad, the advertising ID is only shared if you choose Allow when the app asks about tracking. You can change that any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu varies by phone).

How these companies use the data is covered by Unity's privacy policy. Buying "Remove Ads" turns the ads off completely.

Purchases

Purchases such as "Remove Ads" are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. I never see your payment details; the app only learns whether the purchase was made, so it can turn the ads off.

This website

The site counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you from site to site. I only see totals, such as how many people visited a page and which country they came from.

If you use the light/dark switch, your choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it next time. It never leaves your device, and nothing else is stored.

Children

My apps are for a general audience and aren't directed at children under 13. They don't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new effective date at the top.
