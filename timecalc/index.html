Time Calc

AppAndroidiOS soon

A calculator that understands dates, times and durations.

Use it like any other calculator, then mix in hours, minutes, days and dates. Figure out how long you can stay, how many times that song has played, or how many years 8,766 days really is.

Try it

Leave at 3:54 PM, it's a 37 minute drive each way, and you need to be home by 9. How long can you stay?

(9:00 PM − 3:54 PM) − 2×37m= 3h52m

A 2:30 song has been on repeat for half an hour. How many times has it played?

30m ÷ 2m30s= 12

How long is 8,766 days, really?

8766D= 24Y

With Years turned on in Settings.

What it does

Dates, times and durations

Mix them in one calculation, from years down to seconds, alongside ordinary numbers.

Results in the units you want

Choose which units answers are shown in, from plain days to years, months and weeks.

Keep going

Start a new line with an operator to carry on from the last answer, or drop Ans in anywhere.

Reuse and fix

Copy an earlier line back down with the up arrow, and move the cursor to fix a typo.

Copy/Share results

Copy just the answer or the whole calculation, or share the answer straight to another app.

Nothing to sign up for

No account, no sign-in. Your settings stay on your device.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The free version shows a small banner ad. A one-time Remove Ads purchase, on the About screen, turns it off for good.

I reinstalled the app. How do I get Remove Ads back?

On Android it comes back on its own once you're signed in to the same Google account. On iPhone and iPad, tap Restore on the About screen.

Why are long results shown in days?

Months and years aren't all the same length, so results default to days. If you turn on Months or Years in Settings, Time Calc counts a year as 365.25 days and a month as 30 days, which is close but not exact, so please don't rely on it for anything critical.

How do I see a result in other units?

Open Settings, pick the units you want, then tap =.
